Discover the joy of cargo biking with ***Bakfiet Fam***, a community in Redmond, Washington, dedicated to sharing knowledge and experiences about cargo bikes. Connect with like-minded enthusiasts, learn from our host and site administrator Adron, and embrace the lifestyle with your chosen family of cargo bike aficionados.

Also, just because you may be sans-cargo bike, feel free to join us regardless! We’re a friendly group that love to talk shop and answers questions among prospective new cargo bike riders!
