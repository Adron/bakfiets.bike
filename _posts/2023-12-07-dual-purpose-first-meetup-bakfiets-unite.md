Needless to say, because of the weather (?) the meetup was canceled on account of the underlying event cancellation. For more details, check out Move Redmond’s page [here](https://moveredmond.org/stationaccessmaps/).

*Past post below…*

Coming up on the 13th and 14th of January, in this grand upcoming year of 2024, Move Redmond is organizing an event around *station access mapping* that to me, seems like bakfiets riders really need to be involved in! For more details from the Move Redmond emailer, check out [this link](https://mailchi.mp/1190a9fe8ec3/2023-move-redmond-wrapped?e=54ea8f5bb7).

I signed up for Saturday and Sunday, and wanted to open up the invite and maybe we could even make it a “Bakfiets Rider Gathering” and discuss the station access mapping and future meetups for riders. The specific times and locations I signed up for are…

Sat 1/13 10:00-12:00 @ South Bellevue, then Sunday 1/14 10:00-12:00 @ Overlake Village. I look forward to seeing others there, cheers!
